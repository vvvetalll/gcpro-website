GCPRO Restoration - Marketing Kit
ASP = Agency Support Program (for insurance agents)
PSP = Plumber Support Program (for plumbing companies)
=================================================================

1 Trifolds        - 3 ASP trifold brochures, 11 x 8.5 in, 2 pages (outside / inside).
2 Flyers          - 2 one-page flyers, Letter 8.5 x 11 in.
3 Special Reports - 6 reports for agents, Letter. 01, 02, 03, 05 = 2 pages; 04, 06 = 1 page.
4 Forms           - NonBinding Agreement, Agency Profile, Policy Review, Inspection Invoice.
5 PSP             - Plumber Support Program leave-behind trifold, 11 x 8.5 in.

16 documents + 10 fillable versions = 26 PDFs.

FILLABLE folders = the same documents with form fields you can type into
(Adobe Acrobat / Reader, Mac Preview, Chrome/Edge). Use the regular version for print
and handwriting, the FILLABLE version for filling out on a computer.
Trifolds and flyers have no fillable version: GCPRO contact info is already printed.

Print: all files include 0.125 in bleed - send to print as is, trim to final size.
Colors are RGB (fine for digital print; ask the printer for a proof if they convert to CMYK).
Texts are unchanged from the original kit; only design and structure were rebuilt.
