PROPERTY GUARDIAN PROGRAM
Nobody Knows the Future, But You Can Prepare for It!

The Property Guardian Program (PGP) is a tool for property and facility managers who choose to be proactive rather than reactive when it comes to business disruption or interruption from water, fire or storm damage or the discovery of mold or other biohazards.

The PGP is designed to offer a variety of levels of service and commitment, allowing each property or facility manager to “curate” or customize a program that works best for them and the facilities they are responsible for.

The PGP establishes a relationship between the property or facility manager and a restoration contractor who will function as a Disaster Preparedness Advisor and provide mitigation and restoration services according to the specifics of the PGP.

The Property Guardian Program is ideal for property managers, facility managers, property owners, risk managers and other responsible parties that not only see the value in being proactive but also:

  • Want the fastest, most efficient response and recovery when a loss occurs,
  • Are laser focused on lowering restoration costs, which are typically unbudgeted,
  • Know that outstanding communication is the key to a smoother, lower stress restoration scenario,
  • Value comprehensive documentation to enhance the insurance claims process as well as to protect themselves and all involved parties from future liability and lawsuits,
  • Appreciate vendors that practice discretion to avoid a potential public relations nightmare, and,
  • See why having priority response locked in before a disaster is an essential requirement of their role in protecting the operations of the organization.

Commercial properties are at risk from a wide variety of natural and man-made emergencies such as:

Water damage from floods, freezing, burst pipes, sprinkler heads, appliance failures, sewer backups, etc.
Fire or smoke damage
Severe storms, hurricanes, tornadoes or wind damage
Discovery of mold, trauma scenes or other biohazards

Having a PGP in Place If & When an Emergency Strikes, will:

Contain and minimize the damage and reduce the severity of the loss
Reduce the number of occupants out of the property
Dramatically reduce the possibility of lost tenants
Give property management staff a better understanding and control of the restoration process
Get the property back to normal operations fast, with less stress
Protect the health and safety of employees and occupants
Minimize interruptions to business operations
Prevent or minimize loss of income
Reduce the cost to restore the property
Address Public Relations issues and protect your reputation
Enhance tenant/ owner relations
Protect the public safety
