PROPERTY GUARDIAN PROGRAM
Nobody Knows the Future, But You Can Prepare for It!

The Property Guardian Program (PGP) is a tool for property and facility managers who choose to be proactive rather than reactive when it comes to business disruption or interruption from water, fire or storm damage or the discovery of mold or other biohazards.

The PGP is designed to offer a variety of levels of service and commitment, allowing each property or facility manager to “curate” or customize a program that works best for them and the facilities they are responsible for.

The PGP establishes a relationship between the property or facility manager and a restoration contractor who will function as a Disaster Preparedness Advisor and provide restoration services according to the specifics of the PGP.

The Property Guardian Program is ideal for property managers, facility managers, property owners, risk managers and other responsible parties that not only see the value in being proactive but also:

  • Want the fastest, most efficient response and recovery when a loss occurs,

  • Are laser focused on lowering restoration costs, which are typically unbudgeted,

  • Know that outstanding communication is the key to a smoother, lower stress restoration scenario,

  • Value comprehensive documentation to enhance the insurance claims process as well as to protect themselves and all involved parties from future liability and lawsuits,

  • Appreciate vendors that practice discretion to avoid a potential public relations nightmare, and,

  • See why having priority response locked in before a disaster is an essential requirement of their role in protecting the operations of the organization.

Commercial properties are at risk from a wide variety of natural and man-made emergencies such as:

Water damage from floods, freezing, burst pipes, sprinkler heads, appliance failures, sewer backups, etc.
Fire or smoke damage
Severe storms, hurricanes, tornadoes or wind damage
Discovery of mold, trauma scenes or other biohazards

Having a PGP in Place If & When an Emergency Strikes, will:

Contain and minimize the damage and reduce the severity of the loss
Reduce the number of occupants out of the property
Dramatically reduce the possibility of lost tenants
Give property management staff a better understanding and control of the restoration process
Get the property back to normal operations fast, with less stress
Protect the health and safety of employees and occupants
Minimize interruptions to business operations
Prevent or minimize loss of income
Reduce the cost to restore the property
Address Public Relations issues and protect your reputation
Enhance tenant/ owner relations
Protect the public safety

Upon Request, We Will Provide Emergency Services Only to Stabilize, Mitigate and Prevent Further Damage

  • We will mitigate the loss including board-up and tarping, structural drying, providing restorative cleaning as well as other appropriate actions to stabilize the situation and prevent further damage.

  • To accomplish this we will provide a variety of resources including labor, equipment, materials, tools and other necessary items as needed.

  • We will supply personnel trained in accordance with Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) and the Institute of Inspection Cleaning and Restoration Certification (IICRC) standards.

  • We will have appropriate commercial general liability, pollution liability and workman’s compensation insurance coverage.

  • Our company and our employees will keep all information pertaining to your operations and the emergency situation confidential, unless otherwise required by law or to protect life, health or safety.

NTE/Notes:

We Will Begin Emergency Services Immediately without Requiring a Deposit

  • We will respond, execute a service agreement and begin stabilization and emergency services immediately without an initial deposit being required. This will allow us to respond immediately to losses after hours or on weekends without delay.

Priority Emergency Service

  • Under normal conditions, when an emergency occurs, we will respond to initial notification of the emergency within 15 minutes and will arrive on-site within 2 hours barring extenuating circumstances such as traffic, etc.

  • During a severe, area-wide weather event, our “next available crew” service pledge means that we will provide priority response to all PGP (Preferred) clients with the next available crew before any other non-PGP service requests are fulfilled.

Transparent Communication & Documentation

  • We will provide transparency through comprehensive documentation and regular updates so that any third party such as Regional Managers, distant property owners and Claims Adjusters can see what the source of the loss was and what steps are being taken to restore it. This allows you to stay updated on the progress of restoration without being inconvenienced and creates a permanent record of proper restoration.

  • As your “Claims Administrator” we will help you manage the process and provide the highest levels of communication.

  • As “Mitigation Specialists” our approach will control costs and return you to normal operations faster.

  • Our comprehensive documentation and working to the Standard of Care will help assure you that the work was done correctly and protect you from liability and lawsuits.

Post-Loss Debriefing

  • At your request, we will offer a thorough debriefing after any emergency to review what happened and why, examine whether additional preventive measures should be implemented as well as review the restoration in terms of process, equipment utilized, costs, documentation and other details.

Certificate of Professional Restoration

  • We will provide a certificate of professional restoration that demonstrates that the loss was restored to the IICRC S500 & S520 Standards of Care. A comprehensive documentation packet is also available upon request.

  • This protects the property’s value, protects against “sick building” issues (real or imagined), enhances tenant retention and protects against downstream liability and lawsuits.
