## What the competitors change

There is no universal winning page structure. Romexterra distributes modeled traffic across several service pages; Restore and Chicago Water & Fire remain homepage-heavy. Networks combine real locations with audience/service paths. These observations support meaningful Homepage coverage, useful specialists, clear routing and genuine proof; they do not prove that the most granular sitemap or most links caused success. National domain metrics are not Chicago results, and vendor traffic is not actual lead volume.
