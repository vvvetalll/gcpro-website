## What their observed pages and results suggest

### 1. SERVPRO — local network plus audience/service taxonomy

The Chicago overview links distinct residential and commercial water/fire/mold/storm pathways. Our SERPs also return individual Chicago-franchise service pages, including extraction/drying and pack-out. This is a network of locally routed service destinations, not one Chicago homepage alone. **Inference:** location routing plus service/audience specificity broadens entry points. GCPRO can use clear hub→specialist links and real local proof; it should not imitate a franchise-scale city/service matrix or treat the city-page227 estimate as the entire franchise network's Chicago traffic.

### 2. Romexterra — detailed service clusters with distributed traffic

The public navigation nests extraction, mitigation, flood/sewage, structural drying and other water tasks beneath Water, with smoke/soot/board-up under Fire. Ahrefs' sampled Top Pages distributes modeled traffic across Homepage148, Mold124, Fire Cleanup107, Flood53 and Mitigation33; Homepage share22.5%. **Inference:** several useful service entry pages matter, rather than only a large homepage. The existence of many pages alone does not prove each is worthwhile. For GCPRO, strengthen the five retained supporting routes and parent coverage first, and require unique demand/scope/proof before splitting further.

### 3. ATI — Chicago location hub with local specialty children

Public Chicago office content connects to commercial services and industry pages. Local specialty URLs appear in generic and commercial SERPs. Ahrefs Chicago subtree124 modeled visits is concentrated in Fire/Smoke91, versus office hub16; catastrophe11 and Water3 in the captured rows. **Inference:** local specialist pages can outperform the location index. GCPRO can improve specialist scope and relevant city/project links. ATI's corporate industry taxonomy is not evidence GCPRO needs healthcare, government or industrial landing pages without capabilities and demand.

### 4. Restore Construction — homepage-led authority and proof

Visible site structure includes service pathways, local coverage, projects, credentials, claim-process information, FAQs and glossary. Ahrefs attributes525 modeled visits/91.8% of target traffic to Homepage and27 to Emergency Board-Up. **Inference:** a useful conversion-oriented homepage can retain generic service visibility; splitting every phrase into a dedicated page is not a universal winning rule. GCPRO should retain broad Homepage strength and factual proof while developing specialists. Do not automatically strip every water/fire phrase from Homepage or copy unsupported insurance/certification promises.

### 5. Paul Davis NW Chicago — explicit audience split

The local subdomain exposes parallel Residential/Commercial Water, Fire, Mold and Storm routes, plus commercial large-loss/priority/industry pathways. Commercial Water and Mold appear in our modifier-specific SERPs. Ahrefs local model is small:23 traffic/9 keywords, with Homepage11 and Commercial Water6 in the captured rows; an unrelated top-keyword example also cautions against treating all modeled traffic as qualified demand. **Inference:** the taxonomy demonstrates a plausible audience distinction, not proof that parallel pages automatically produce high local traffic. GCPRO's retained split needs actual differentiated copy and proof.

### 6. BluSky — commercial service depth on a larger brand

Chicago location content links commercial emergency response, water/fire/mold/storm, board-up, contents, environmental work and roofing. Separate root-level Chicago fire/mold pages appear in our SERPs outside the measured location prefix. **Inference:** a commercial positioning layer plus specialty routes supports business-intent discovery. GCPRO can improve property-manager/access/scope information; it should not infer that copying environmental or roofing service lists creates competence, or treat the local-prefix41 estimate as a company-wide performance measure.

### 7. Chicago Water & Fire — strong homepage plus educational support

The site links water-related material/problem pages, Contents Storage and Commercial Services. Ahrefs Homepage758/share68.8% is the dominant row; sampled articles contribute Mold/Mildew80, Water Shutoff77, Mold Causes36 and Moisture Mapping31. **Inference:** transactional entry and selected informational coverage work together; article traffic may be broad/nonlocal and is not necessarily an emergency lead. For GCPRO, maintain one transactional owner and use focused helpful resources/project proof to support it. Do not mistake national educational traffic for local commercial demand.

### 8. ServiceMaster DSI Chicago — local audience/service subtree

The local site visibly separates commercial water/fire/mold/storm and residential services. Ahrefs reports38 keywords/123 modeled visits for the chosen subtree. Captured entry pages include Commercial Water27, Residential Mold16, Flooded Basement16 and other specialist work. **Inference:** narrow loss/problem pages can have value when they answer different tasks, but not every competitor capability belongs in GCPRO's scope. The local-prefix sample excludes other ServiceMaster franchises and can omit the slashless hub itself; it is not the entire brand's Chicago footprint.

### 9. Midway Restoration — relevant architecture, weak vendor coverage

The public site separates audience hubs and residential water/fire/mold/reconstruction/contents/document paths. Our SERPs return its residential contents, fire and commercial hub pages. Ahrefs captures only2 keywords/1 modeled visit, despite this observed presence. **Inference:** this is a useful architecture comparison, not evidence of superior measured results. Do not merge it with Midway Building Services, which has a separate domain and SERP footprint. Copying a broad list of specialisms is not itself an SEO plan.

### 10. BELFOR Northbrook — local establishment backed by national expertise

The Northbrook page identifies the office and links residential emergency/water/fire/mold/storm plus contents and specialist recovery services. Our commercial/contents SERPs show BELFOR local-office entries; Chicago West is a separate route. **Inference:** a real establishment with relevant corporate service paths can compete without a separate Northbrook page for every niche. GCPRO can strengthen its truthful Lombard identity and project evidence; it should neither invent offices nor copy national specialist capabilities. Ahrefs' chosen-prefix0 remains a coverage limitation.

## Consequences for GCPRO's draft strategy

1. **Finish useful specialists and actual links before expansion.** Homepage currently has unresolved `#` actions; hub specialist links work. Request separate implementation approval, then connect verified native destinations and build water/fire/mold pages with actual scope/proof.
2. **Keep Homepage commercially strong.** Romexterra/Restore/CWF show different mixes of homepage versus specialist entry. The initial generic→Residential allocation is superseded: [MARKET_ARCHITECTURE.md](MARKET_ARCHITECTURE.md) now recommends audience-neutral service roles from independent evidence. Keep short meaningful water/fire summaries and reevaluate ownership with query/page evidence after an approved build. No automatic redirect/retitling decision follows from competitors.
3. **Use the existing audience split where intent supports it.** Commercial water/fire/mold have demand, distinct SERP pages and stakeholder needs. Contents has more overlap; preserve its existing conversion route and avoid overinvestment until actual enquiries and capability support it.
4. **Do not clone the largest sitemap.** Romexterra's detailed water techniques are useful research prompts; existing root Extraction/Board-Up/Mapping/Odor pages can cover tasks without a new URL for every synonym. Structural Drying stays a substantial Water section initially; reassess after evidence, not merely because a competitor has a page.
5. **Use honest local and project evidence.** Strengthen one Lombard establishment and confirmed city coverage. Build evidence-rich local pages in measured waves, not51 interchangeable pages or imaginary offices.
6. **Develop corroboration selectively.** Audit current citations, relevant restoration/industry relationships, real project stories and legitimate local editorial opportunities. No outreach, account creation, link purchase or publication was authorized or performed.
7. **Judge results with the right source.** Use GSC query/page clicks/impressions and qualified lead outcomes for GCPRO; Ahrefs for comparative estimates and discovery. Review changes using consistent scope/date/settings. Vendor content/AI labels, low KD or backlink totals are not a recipe or causal proof.
