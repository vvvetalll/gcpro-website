### Commercial Restoration Questions

- **eyebrow:** 07 / PROPERTY MANAGER QUESTIONS
- **description:** Practical starting points for property managers, facility teams and business owners planning restoration work.

#### What do commercial restoration services include?

Commercial restoration can include damage assessment, water removal and drying, fire and smoke cleanup, mold remediation, building repairs, reconstruction and restoration of affected contents. The services needed depend on the property conditions and agreed scope. Use the six service categories above to explore the relevant work.

#### Can the business remain open during restoration?

That depends on the damage, affected areas, access and the requirements of the building. Discuss operational needs during assessment. Any decisions about occupancy, restricted areas or reopening need to be made with the responsible property team and appropriate authorities; uninterrupted operations cannot be assumed.

#### What information should I provide when I call?

Share the property address, what happened, which areas are affected and who can authorize access. Details about tenants, operating hours and other project contacts help the initial conversation. Provide photos only when they can be taken safely, and tell us about any known access restrictions.

#### How is the project schedule determined?

The schedule depends on the extent of damage, drying or cleanup needs, agreed repairs, material availability and access to the affected areas. Discuss priorities and constraints with our team. A realistic sequence of work can be developed after the property and scope have been assessed.

#### How do reconstruction and general contracting differ?

Reconstruction addresses rebuilding after property damage. General contracting can also cover planned commercial repairs, renovations and remodeling. Some projects involve both. Our team can discuss which service fits the condition of your building and the improvements included in your proposed scope.

#### Can damaged business contents be restored?

Some furniture and other business items may be suitable for cleaning or restoration. Options depend on the material, type of damage and condition of each item. Ask about evaluation, packing and storage needs; not every item will be recoverable.
