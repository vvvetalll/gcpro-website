## Important observations and conflicts

GSC selected three-month property totals were481 clicks,93,243 impressions, displayed CTR0.5%, position31; latest-update UI said4.5hours ago when inspected. These are site search-performance measures, not monthly market demand. Query tables are truncated by the UI/top-row limit and privacy/aggregation; do not expect row sums to reproduce every chart total.

Keywords Explorer displays water damage restoration Chicago SV700/KD2/CPC$5, whereas Site Explorer's September15 comparison row displays800/62/$5.28 and **Lost**. Preserve both observations; do not reconcile by averaging or presenting either as guaranteed low difficulty. Ahrefs also displayed a September29 notice about Google serving changes and possible SERP inconsistencies. Vendor refresh periods differ and the underlying monthly estimation window was not exposed. Zero KD is a vendor value, not proof of no competition.

National commercial fire4.3K/water2.9K/mold2.4K cannot be used as Chicago demand. Chicago-modified queries were0–10/10/60 respectively; Planner's city-filtered **unmodified commercial service** terms show10–100 ranges. These are different dimensions and keyword forms, not conflicting local totals. Residential water-Chicago40 was measured separately; residential fire/mold/hub exact phrases were not indexed, so no values were invented for them.

The master map uses one explicitly named `measurement_keyword`; for city pages it is the water-service **secondary comparator**, while the primary cluster is broad restoration-service intent. The notes flag this difference. Metrics for other secondary variations stay in the raw/normalized keyword files; a comparator's volume must never be attributed to every variation in the cluster. A blank measurement cell is unknown. Displayed$0.00 CPC remains a vendor observation, not a conclusion of no business value.

## Prioritization, coverage and validation

P1/P2/P3 are editorial work-sequencing judgments. Inputs: verified service relevance, audience/task distinction, observed demand, existing GSC opportunity, project proof/readiness and overlap. No revenue, closed-lead quality, average job value, margin or capacity dataset was available; no ROI model or ranking forecast is claimed. Generic high-demand markets can be competitive despite low vendor KD; weaker-volume precise tasks may be feasible but are not guaranteed quick wins.

Every CSV row has a unique identifier; allocated primary clusters have exactly one preferred target. Blank primaries on support/hold/legacy rows are intentional. All nonblank primary phrases are unique. Supporting task ownership and deferrals are in the supporting analysis, not new unapproved URLs. Generic roles are selected independently as audience-neutral; the preservation-driven Residential allocation is superseded. Historical briefs were preserved; project references were updated rather than duplicated. Planned vs implemented states and source periods remain separate. Validation checks parse CSV/JSON, compare map/brief counts and service/city coverage, resolve local Markdown links, review secrets/sensitive fields and confirm the remote documentation-only commit. Validation results are recorded in [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md).

Raw browser account chrome, lead/customer rows, billing details and credentials are not research artifacts. The captured datasets are reviewed business search queries/metrics and public/staging page metadata only. No Ads campaign, ad group, saved keyword or budget action was taken; Keyword Planner discovery settings were used for research. No website writes or navigation changes were made.
