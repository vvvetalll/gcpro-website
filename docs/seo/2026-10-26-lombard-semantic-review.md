Before launch: settle the three article roles, maintain consistent descriptive internal links, strengthen supported local evidence, and obtain a Search Console query/page comparison for the relevant Lombard cluster. Keep current self-canonical/noindex staging settings; do not canonicalize the distinct city page to Homepage as a shortcut.

Primary guidance: [Google doorway-abuse policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#doorway-abuse) distinguishes substantially similar location/funnel pages; it does not prohibit useful local pages. [Google canonical guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls) addresses duplicate/very similar URLs and consistent internal links; it is not a substitute for differentiating genuine page purposes.
