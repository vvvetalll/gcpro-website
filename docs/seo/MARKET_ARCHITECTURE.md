# Independent market-led semantic architecture

**DRAFT — AWAITING USER APPROVAL**

This is the controlling proposal for the semantic strategy, revised2026-10-21 following the owner's clarification. **Search tasks, market demand and independent SERPs determine page roles first.** Legacy pages, URLs, historical keyword assignments and GSC are diagnostic evidence and later implementation/migration inputs. They do not establish the correct architecture. No URL, content, menu, redirect or publication change has been made or approved.

## Method and evidence order

1. Identify the searcher's job: broad company selection, specific damage restoration, explicit commercial scope, narrow supporting task, local provider or referral/conversion.
2. Compare exact query forms and geography without summing synonyms: US city-modified Ahrefs estimates, separately Chicago-city-filtered Planner ranges and actual visible Google page types.
3. Test generic/residential/commercial variants against independent results and available demand. Similar words alone do not create a separate market; audience differences must produce useful distinct content.
4. Assign one preferred semantic owner, or mark the role conditional/unsupported. Only then compare legacy pages and GSC to identify useful material, current visibility, overlap, missing routes and transition risk.
5. Decide implementation/reuse and exact URLs after owner approval. Proposed paths below are illustrative handles for intent roles, not approved destinations. A neutral role might later reuse an existing object/path if the reviewed tradeoffs support it; it does not require six new pages by default.

Evidence includes119 unique Ahrefs probes, Chicago Planner ranges,20 initial multi-service SERPs and11 further independent checks: Residential Water/Fire/Mold plus eight suburban water markets. GSC is consulted after these signals as a diagnostic; it is not used to manufacture demand or to bless the prior targeting.

## Explicit challenges to previous targeting

- **Generic does not automatically mean Residential.** The first working draft allocated generic clusters to Residential paths for preservation. That logic is superseded. Generic service roles are audience-neutral; a legacy label is not intent evidence.
- **Residential and Commercial are not symmetric SEO trees.** Independent residential checks overlap generic results heavily. Commercial Water/Mold and selected Fire/Reconstruction roles have stronger modifier/task evidence. Useful audience navigation can exist without a separate SEO landing for each audience/service permutation.
- **Existing Commercial Contents is not automatically a justified SEO owner.** Start with one combined Contents role and business scope; assess a distinct conversion need before retaining an independent commercial sales target. No removal or redirect is authorized.
- **Existing Dehumidification is not automatically a justified standalone target.** Initially cover the process on Water; evaluate existing content/links/history later. Market ambiguity and parent overlap matter more than route existence.
- **Fifty-one legacy cities are not fifty-one proven SEO opportunities.** Nine receive conditional targeting after demand/SERP checks;42 remain unallocated pending local validation. A city-modified water estimate is not broad-city restoration volume or service coverage confirmation.
- **Current ranking URL is not necessarily the desired owner.** Homepage's historical/current water visibility is a diagnostic asset/risk, not proof it must permanently own the generic water task. Conversely, successful competitor homepages mean a specialist allocation is not guaranteed superior; preserve meaningful Homepage coverage and test the approved strategy against consistent query/page outcomes.
