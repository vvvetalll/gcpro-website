## Decision thresholds

Create a new page only after confirming current delivery, a materially different search task, distinct useful content/proof and demand or meaningful conversion value. A low-volume service can deserve a page; a high-volume but unoffered service does not. For existing routes, preserve until separately approved URL actions. Commercial revenue and lead quality were not available, so priorities are editorial and must be reviewed with the owner.
