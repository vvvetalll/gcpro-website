## What the former work got right

Preserving exact URLs pending approval, separating navigation from semantic ownership, avoiding automatic city publication, consolidating drying into the water task, retaining distinct extraction/board-up/odor/moisture support roles where useful, refusing to fabricate local offices, and treating the strategy as a draft are all sound. The existing Residential and Commercial hubs have approved designs and reusable Atomic components; competitor research does not justify discarding that investment.

## What changed materially

1. **Homepage concentration is measurable.** Restore's homepage has 553 of approximately 601 estimated visits, while its water service page has 3. CWF's homepage has 758; Romexterra's meaningful service-page contribution differs. “Copy their page count” is not an evidence-based strategy.
2. **Contents is a distinct workflow, not a synonym list.** Observed ranking pages describe inventory, packout, cleaning, storage and return. GCPRO needs facts about its own handling capabilities before a meaningful Contents brief can be implemented.
3. **Commercial demand is supported by mixed evidence.** ATI commercial and Paul commercial pages occur in live results; Paul’s broad local homepage also owns an Ahrefs commercial query. A separate B2B page is justified by a distinct user task and operational content, not keyword modifier alone.
4. **Local relevance is not national DR.** SERVPRO/BluSky regional scopes can show modest estimated traffic while ranking repeatedly; other franchise URLs lie outside those scopes. BELFOR's local prefix shows zero in Ahrefs yet appears for Contents. Zero sampled estimates do not establish absence from Google.
5. **Midway is not Midway Building Services.** Both appear separately in commercial results; Ahrefs even assigns a Midway Building Services query to Midway Restoration. That is keyword/entity ambiguity, not proof that the companies are one business.
6. **An endpoint can rank in stale data while currently failing.** Excel's `/Service` was estimated at six visits in Top Pages but returned HTTP404. Investigate live state; do not reproduce historical URLs blindly.
7. **GCPRO’s brand dominates the small Ahrefs baseline.** The brand is assigned mainly to About; Bridgeport→Bridgeview is a geographic relevance warning. This does not overturn GSC’s broader historical impressions.
