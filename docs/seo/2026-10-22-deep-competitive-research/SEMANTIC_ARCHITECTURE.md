The service families remain useful; six additional root URLs are unnecessary in this implementation phase. The observations support intent coverage, not a ranking advantage from a shallower URL. Do not create parallel `/water-damage-restoration/`, `/fire-damage-restoration/`, `/mold-remediation/`, `/storm-damage-restoration/`, `/reconstruction/` or `/contents-restoration/` pages to duplicate these owners. This rejects automatic new-page creation, not any already existing historical production URL: preserve every inventory row and do not redirect/delete anything under this recommendation.

Commercial Fire 520, Reconstruction 524 and Contents 525 retain existing routes but receive no automatic duplicate SEO builds. General Contracting 522 remains its distinct business scope if offered. Require a materially different buyer task, real capabilities and useful evidence before extensive commercial child content. Residential means household recovery; commercial means stakeholders, access, records and operating constraints. A modifier alone does not justify two interchangeable bodies.

Preserve all 51 objects; do not bulk publish, remove or create city×service copies. No fabricated Glenview office or service radius.

## Evidence and informational content

Use Gallery 493 `/before-and-after/` and existing Portfolio 1132 `/portfolio/` to expose two documented cases. New case entries are proposed content, exact IDs/slugs unassigned; no parallel gallery system. First case: Glenview water work if records confirm it. Second: actual fire or mold job selected from available owner records, not an invented location or testimonial.

Defer standalone mold-vs-mildew, shutoff, frozen-pipe and similar articles until core service pages and evidence are complete. National informational volume is not Chicago lead demand. Moisture diagnostics can first strengthen 490/514. Exclude jobs, competitor-branded, medical, asbestos and unrelated sinkhole queries from the production queue; no unsupported service claims.

## Internal-link and overlap policy

Home → hubs plus primary service owners; 484/499 → relevant services; generic services → distinct business sibling, support task, genuine case, 495 and 492; cases → performed service and truthful city; city → relevant service and case. Use existing native Elementor object links, shared components/classes/variables. Preserve Why 1149, Process 1152, Areas 1140, CTA 80 and approved hub layouts. No new component library, CSS/JS dependency or redesign is justified.

Choose one editorial primary per intent; a secondary homepage ranking is not proof of cannibalization. Repeated query-to-page attribution and outcome evidence would be needed before consolidation. Migration remains deferred: exact production history, indexing/canonical, GSC attribution and backlinks are insufficient for a safe move in this package. The old draft's root-page recommendation is superseded for this phase; historical documents/data remain intact.
