# GCPRO competitive SEO intelligence — 22 October 2026

**Research only. Recommendations await approval. No WordPress, Elementor, URL, redirect, publication, or SEO-setting changes were made.** This dated package supplements and preserves the 21 October research; it does not silently replace the draft keyword map.

## Decision for the next build

Improve the existing service inventory before creating a new parallel inventory. The first implementation package should make Water 514, Fire/Smoke 517 and Mold 516 useful destination pages, connect them from Homepage 154 and the existing audience hubs, and attach two documented local projects. Develop the distinct business workflow on Commercial Water 515 and Commercial Mold 519 only with operational information from GCPRO. Keep Commercial 499 and Residential 484; refine them instead of rebuilding their approved sections.

**Reconsider the earlier automatic move toward six new neutral root service URLs.** Competitors succeed with homepages, regional hubs, audience paths and specialty pages. The observations justify complete service coverage and clear intent ownership; they do not establish that a root URL outperforms an existing nested URL. A generic service owner can initially use an existing nested route. Any future path change needs its own production URL/GSC/backlink review and approval.

Home 154 should retain broad Chicago property-restoration positioning and concise emergency/service orientation. It can attract water/fire searches as secondary intents, as competitor homepages do. “One primary owner” is an editorial linking policy, not a claim that Google must show only one URL or that two ranking URLs prove cannibalization.

## Most decision-relevant new evidence

* Romexterra: US estimate 668 visits/month; homepage 148, mold page 124, fire-cleanup page 107. Its service pages demonstrably contribute; its huge city/service menu is not evidence that every combination works.
* Restore: US estimate 601; homepage 553 (92.2%). A large menu and many location/service pages do not imply distributed organic success. Generic US “disaster restoration” traffic is not measured Chicago demand.
* CWF: US estimate 1.1K; homepage 758, mold/mildew guide 87, shutoff guide 77. Informational reach exists, but these article visits cannot be treated as local emergency leads.
* ATI’s Chicago commercial page and Paul Davis’s commercial hub appear in the business-query observations. ServiceMaster DSI’s commercial-water URL has estimated traffic 27 across five keywords. This supports business-specific workflow content, not six automatic duplicate B2B service pages.
* Glenview results include SERVPRO of Glenview, Romexterra’s city hub, Restoration 1, North Shore Restoration, United Services, Tomkat and ServiceMaster by Elite. A single Glenview page with GCPRO’s real Glenview project is more defensible than publishing all 51 city objects.
* GCPRO’s US estimate is only 20, dominated by the brand query. A Bridgeport keyword is attributed to a Bridgeview URL: investigate geographic intent before repeating city templates.

These are Ahrefs estimates or localized observations, not revenue forecasts. Details and source report URLs are retained in the CSVs. The 30-day plan is an implementation proposal, not authorization to execute it.
